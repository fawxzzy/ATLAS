# Atlas UI Standards Marker Admission

Date: `2026-07-15`

## Decision

Admit `lane-atlas-ui-standards-adoption` as a percentage-null backlog candidate in the Atlas full-system re-evaluation registry.

## Measurement Boundary

- Percentage: `null`
- Denominator: `UNKNOWN`
- Measurement unit: accepted owner-profile proof unit
- Root configuration does not count as owner adoption.
- Candidate packets do not count as live cards or completed work.
- Unknown, waived, or warning-only evidence does not count as completion.

## Owner Boundary

Atlas owns the standards, profiles, schemas, and marker policy. Each owner repository retains its routes, components, tokens, runtime, accessibility implementation, and repo-native verification. DiscordOS owns any later live card projection and exact readback.

## Initial Candidate Owners

The existing root foundation records candidate packets for Fitness, Trove, Mazer, Socials OS, Stream, Nat1 Games, DiscordOS UI, and Playbook review. Each remains unplanned until its owner accepts applicability and scope.

## Dependencies

Live card projection depends on the cross-project DiscordOS board-integrity lane. Playbook promotion depends on repeated verified evidence and separate doctrine review. Neither dependency is satisfied by this admission alone.

## Marker Effect

No marker moved. The admission adds one explicitly tracked candidate lane so the closing full-system audit cannot lose the UI standards program or misclassify its root foundation as stack-wide adoption.
