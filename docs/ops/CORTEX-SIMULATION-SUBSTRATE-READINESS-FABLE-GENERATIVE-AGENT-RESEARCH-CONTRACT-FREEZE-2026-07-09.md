## What Generative-Agent Research Contributes Architecturally

The Generative Agents paper contributes the stronger architecture pattern:

- memory of prior experience
- reflection over that experience
- retrieval of the right memories at decision time
- planning against current state
- sandboxed interaction among agents
- emergent individual and social behavior

That is the strongest substrate cue for Cortex Simulation because it points to governed scenario reasoning rather than one-shot content generation.
