# Fawxzzy Sage Theme Mapping

## Requested anchors
- UI box background black-green: `#070D08`
- Dusty sage text: `#8BA888`
- Light button and highlight sage: `#A3BCAC`

## Clean role mapping
- Primary text: `#8BA888`
- Secondary text: `#A3BCAC`
- Strong text and light neutral: `#D6E3D2`
- Main background: `#070D08`
- Elevated surfaces: `#0A100B`, `#0E150F`, `#111912`
- Borders: `#4A604C`, `#6F866D`
- Brown undertones: `#31291F`, `#564838`

## Legacy color replacements
- Old blue and info: `#7F9D98`
- Old purple: `#879786`
- Old green and success: `#8BA888`
- Old orange and warning: `#948D6F`
- Old red and danger: `#967B7B`
- Old bright neutral: `#D6E3D2`

## Motion direction
Use a dark forest base with slow sage glow, soft particles, and restrained earth undertones. The motion should feel premium and atmospheric, not neon and not noisy.

## Adoption rule
Keep the green and earth channels separated. Do not tint the whole app brown, and do not wash every surface with bright green.
