# Playbook product roadmap (ownership pointer)

## Fact

ATLAS does not own the Playbook product roadmap. It lives in `fawxzzy/playbook` (`docs/PLAYBOOK_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`, `docs/roadmap/ROADMAP.json`). ATLAS stack work is tracked in `docs/programs/ATLAS-MASTER-PROGRAM-REGISTER.v1.source.json` and [backlog/STACK-DEBT-BURNDOWN.md](backlog/STACK-DEBT-BURNDOWN.md).

## Interpretation
